THE CONCEPT & CULTURAL ROOTS

More than just a server. An informal digital sanctuary.

In Kerala, every town and village has a Thattukada. It is where people from every walk of life step under the tin roof during torrential monsoons, holding a glass of steaming black sulaimani, trading stories, and feeling instantly connected.

We brought that exact unpretentious warmth to Discord. Whether you are living in Kochi, Bengaluru, Dubai, London, or Toronto, Thattukada is your home away from home — open 24 hours with a warm bench waiting for you.

COMMUNITY CODE

The House Rules

A harmonious tea stall requires mutual respect. Here are the core values we uphold together.

01.

Respect the Table

Treat every member with fundamental dignity and warmth. No hate speech, discrimination, harassment, or personal attacks under any circumstance.

02.

Keep the Banter Warm & Safe

Humor and playful teasing are part of Kerala teashop culture, but keep it friendly and consensual. Know when to step back if someone is uncomfortable.

03.

No Spam or Unsolicited Promotion

Keep self-promotion and external links to dedicated channels (#showcase). Keep discussions focused on building genuine human connections.

04.

Language Inclusivity

Both Malayalam (Malayalam script or Manglish) and English are welcomed. Never mock anyone for their language proficiency, dialect, or background.
